Data Analysis Key Findings
Q1:
The  N=20000  configuration came closest to ideal scaling at 2 processes but failed to maintain it. 
For all other configurations and higher process counts, the speedup remained significantly below the ideal linear line.

Q2: 
The divergence occurs sharply at 4 processes across all sizes, where efficiency drops
- The use of --oversubscribe is a major factor: as processes exceed physical core counts 
context switching and resource contention for CPU cache/memory bandwidth dominate.
- For small N (1000), communication overhead is higher than the computation time, 
leading to immediate efficiency loss even at 2 processes.

Q3:
Larger N improves efficiency because the computational workload becomes the dominant factor 
over MPI overhead.

Q4: 
N is limited by the system's RAM. leading to MemoryError or Segmentation Faults during allocation.
